
# Street Food Finder & Rating App

## About
Street Food Finder & Rating is a mobile application that helps users discover nearby street food vendors. Users can view vendors on an interactive map, explore menus, get directions, rate food, write reviews, and upload photos.

The app also helps vendors promote their businesses and connect with customers and suppliers.

## User Roles
- **Customers:** Tourists, travelers, students, and workers.
- **Vendors:** Street food sellers who manage their profiles, menus, and promotions.
- **Suppliers:** Suppliers who connect with vendors and manage their profiles.

## Key Features
- User registration, login, and logout.
- Interactive map showing nearby food vendors.
- Vendor profile and menu management.
- Supplier profile management.
- Food ratings, reviews, and photos.
- Search by dish name or ingredients.
- Offline access to saved locations and menus.
- Accessibility settings, including dark mode, light mode, and screen reader support.
